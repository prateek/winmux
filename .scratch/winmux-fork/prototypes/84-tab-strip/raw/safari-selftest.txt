AXHeading | What each choice asks of you
AXStaticText | What each choice asks of you
AXStaticText | Without a grip:
AXStaticText |  Today and Thin retain empty strip space and the 28-point trailing gutter as whole-group drag targets. Held can use those while revealed; at rest it uses the sidebar header. Sidebar uses that header only. The native sidebar header already drags a whole group.
AXStaticText | Without a strip at rest:
AXStaticText |  Held needs Option or sidebar hover to expose membership and pointer tab selection. Sidebar always needs rail hover. The collapsed native rail shows only the workspace number, so neither communicates the group while collapsed.
AXStaticText | Beside a browser:
AXStaticText |  Thin leaves two rows, but separates the app tabs from the quieter group labels. Held and Sidebar remove the duplicate row at rest, trading away immediate group visibility. Neither puts WinMux tabs into an app’s title bar.
AXStaticText | Height is a feature change:
AXStaticText |  the config parser rejects 20 or less, and the runtime floors every accepted height at 36. The 24-point look needs the runtime floor changed. A future feature reuses the existing hint reducer/controller seam for the 500 ms reveal.
AXStaticText | The sidebar prototype has since shipped as “The sidebar as a tree”. This page uses captures of that native sidebar. Orca is skipped because it is Prateek’s own tool and is not installable in this guest.
