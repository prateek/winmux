Settled in round 1

Lift for the selection. The card as the default tile. Real size in the grid. Sections flow by default, and each Presentation takes its own arrangement in the config. Tiles shrink to fit; paging comes later. Grouping is by workspace by default, set in the config, and changed while the Lens is open from the control above the panel.

What I need you to pick now

1. Peek

Space to zoom is Quick Look for windows: the picture grows out of its tile to the window's real size and place, over the dimmed grid. Arrows move to the next window while it stays up. Space or Esc puts it back. Follows selection is AltTab's: the window sits under the panel, which only works when the panel is small.Recommended: Space to zoom everywhere, with follows-selection as an option for the strip

2. Hints

While Option is held: the Summon label on the selected tile, where it would land, the workspace a number key sends each tile to, and a legend of the Lens's keys. The panel thins so the landing spot shows through.Recommended: held, with Option as the default key

3. With no paging, when tiles get very small

Try 42 windows. They keep shrinking and nothing is cut off. The other choice is a floor, with the rest counted as "+N" and out of reach until paging exists.Recommended: keep shrinking

4. The grouping key

The control above the panel is clickable, and one key cycles it. I used cmd-G, which also works while cmd is held in a cmd-tab strip.Recommended: cmd-G, changeable in the Lens's keys
