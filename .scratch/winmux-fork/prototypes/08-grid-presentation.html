Grid Presentation prototype

Throwaway prototype for Prototype: grid Presentation look and behaviour (ticket 08). One grid for the overview Lens, where every design choice is its own switch. The switches are numbered to match the breakdown of choices. Presets A–D recreate the first round's four variants, so start from one and change one thing at a time.

How to drive it
  • Keys (click the screen first): the arrow keys move the selection, following switch 10. ↩ focuses. Hold ⇧ to see the Summon affordance, and ⇧↩ Summons. tab moves to the next section. ⌥W closes a window and ⌥1–8 moves it to that workspace; the Lens binds ⌘W and ⌘1–8, but the browser takes those. Typing filters, ⌫ edits the filter, and esc clears it. [ and ] cycle the presets.
  • Mouse: hovering selects, a click focuses, a ⇧-click Summons, and the scroll wheel pages. Focusing a window switches the current workspace, so you can watch the live and frozen states move.
  • Thumbnail states are the measured ones. Windows on the current workspace are live. Everywhere else they're frozen at park time, because Chrome, Electron and Metal apps stop painting once covered. Safari keeps painting, so its thumbnails are fresh. A hidden app's window shows the capture taken before the hide, or the app icon if nothing was captured.
  • Sharing a combination: the URL carries every setting. Copy it with the button in the State panel and paste it back to me as your answer.
