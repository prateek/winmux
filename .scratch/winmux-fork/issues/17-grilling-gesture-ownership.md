# Grilling: which trackpad gestures WinMux owns

Type: grilling
Status: open
Blocked by: 16

## Question

Should WinMux require the conflicting system swipes to be turned off (the research's recommendation, possibly done by WinMux or dotfiles via `defaults write`), or leave them on and rely on the experimental DockSwipe suppressor? Which three- and four-finger directions does WinMux take over? What stays native (e.g. horizontal swipe between full-screen apps)? And what happens to the Dock's Mission Control once WinMux's grid replaces it?
